Bank Transaction Monitoring
Duration: 2 hours

Scenario
A bank wants to automate transaction monitoring in Odoo.

Develop a solution that runs periodically in the           background and:

Flags transactions of ₦10 million or more for review.
Detects customers who make more than five               transactions within 10 minutes.
Processes normal transactions while preventing               duplicate processing.
Handles errors and processes large volumes of               transactions efficiently.
Task
Translate this business requirement into a working Odoo  implementation. You are free to design the models, fields, business logic, and scheduled actions as you consider  appropriate. Explain your approach and key design decisions.

Scoring
Total: 30 marks

You have two hours to complete the assessment. Rate  yourself honestly in each area based on the quality and  completeness of your implementation.
